Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data we process when you use the Venera Studios website, create an account, join our Discord server, or order from us. It is written from what our systems actually do, and we update it when they change.

Updated 24 October 2026Version 2026-10-24Next review before the store opens

Controller details

The data controller is the operator named below and in the Legal notice. Those details are being finalised and will be published before the Tebex store opens.

In short#

  • The website has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies.
  • If you create an account, we keep only what the account needs. You can download your data or delete the account yourself, on the account page.
  • On our Discord server, a bot runs tickets, quotes, moderation and server security. It keeps only what those functions need, and some of it is deleted automatically.
  • We never sell personal data and never use it for advertising.

Payments#

  • Store purchases are processed by Tebex as the reseller (merchant of record). Tebex collects and processes your payment data as an independent controller under its own privacy policy. We receive from Tebex the order details we need to deliver the product and support you, for example what was bought, when, and the account or server it was linked to.
  • Custom orders are paid as stated in your quote. We keep the payment records that bookkeeping and tax law require.
  • We never receive or store your card details.

3. Automated moderation#

To protect our Discord servers, the bot reacts automatically when it detects a raid or a destructive action:

  • During a detected raid (many joins within seconds), accounts younger than 7 days that join are removed from the server, and new messages are paused until it is calm again.
  • An account that deletes, bans or changes things on a scale that matches an attack has its roles removed and is banned, and the changes are restored.

Every such event is reported to our staff. If you think you were affected by mistake, open a ticket (or ask a member to open one for you) and a person will look at it.

7. Your rights#

You have the right to:

  • access the data we hold about you;
  • rectify wrong data;
  • erase data, where there is no reason to keep it;
  • restrict processing;
  • data portability, a copy in a common format;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interest;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent.

On the account page you can download your data and delete your account yourself. For anything else, write to or open a ticket on Discord. We answer within one month. We may need to confirm it is really you before we act.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU country where you live, where you work, or where you think the law was broken. In Slovenia this is the Information Commissioner (ip-rs.si↗); in Germany it is the data protection authority of the federal state.

8. Security#

  • The website and all connections to it use HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored only as hashes by our authentication provider. New accounts must confirm their email address.
  • Our database allows each signed-in user to read and change only their own records.
  • The bot's data is stored on a server that accepts only key-based administrator access and blocks repeated failed sign-in attempts. The data folder is readable only by the system administrator account.
  • Ticket logs and transcripts are visible only to the ticket owner and staff.

No system is perfectly secure. If you find a security problem, please tell us in a ticket.

9. Children#

Our services are not directed at children under 16. A website account requires that you are at least 16, or have a parent's or guardian's consent. Discord sets its own minimum age for its platform.

10. Changes to this policy#

When our processing changes, we update this policy and the date at the top. If a change matters for your account, we tell you on the website or on Discord.
