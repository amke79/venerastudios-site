Working with us

Frequently asked questions

Short answers, with a link to the full page where there is one.

Updated 24 October 2026

About us#

What does Venera Studios do?#

FiveM scripts made to order, Discord servers built from zero or reworked, and video editing. See About the studio.

Who is legally behind Venera Studios?#

The operator named in the Legal notice. Venera Studios itself is a brand name.

Are you connected to Rockstar Games, Cfx.re, Discord or Tebex?#

No. We use their platforms, but we are not approved, sponsored or endorsed by them. See the Third-party disclaimer.

Ordering and payment#

How do I order?#

Open a ticket on our Discord, fill in the short form, and accept the written quote. See Ordering and quotes.

When does the store open?#

Soon. We do not give a date we cannot keep. The opening is announced in the announcements channel on Discord.

How long does an order take?#

It depends on the size of the job. Your quote states the estimated delivery time.

Can I ask for changes?#

Yes. The quote says how many revision rounds are included. Bigger changes are quoted separately before we start them.

Can I get a refund?#

It depends on the product and on how far the work has got, and your statutory rights always apply. See Refunds and cancellation.

Scripts#

Which frameworks do your scripts support?#

ESX, QBCore, Qbox and ox_core, or standalone, through our bridge layer. Each product page or quote lists what it was built and tested for. See FiveM scripts.

Can I share or resell a script I bought?#

No. You get a licence to use it on the number of servers stated for the product, not the right to pass it on. See Licensing and intellectual property.

Do you help with installation?#

Yes, in your ticket. Scripts come with installation steps. See Support.

Data and safety#

What data do you keep about me?#

Only what the service needs. See the Privacy Policy. The website uses no tracking cookies, see the Cookie Policy.

Will staff ever ask for my password?#

Never. We never ask for passwords, tokens, licence keys or payment details in direct messages. If someone does, it is not us. Report it in a ticket.
