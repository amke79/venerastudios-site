Cookie Policy

The Venera Studios website uses no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It sets no cookies. It stores a few small values in your browser, and only for things you asked for. This page lists every one of them.

Updated 24 October 2026Version 2026-10-24Next review whenever a new tool is added to the website

What the website stores#

Checked against the website's code on 24 October 2026.

NameTypeSet whenPurposeKept
sb-…-auth-token and related sb-… entriesLocal storageYou sign inKeeps you signed in. Set by our authentication library (Supabase)Until you sign out

The website sets no cookies. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) does not set cookies on our pages either.

Why there is no consent banner#

The law requires consent before a website stores or reads information on your device, unless that storage is strictly necessary to provide a service you explicitly asked for (Article 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive 2002/58/EC, implemented for example in Article 225 of the Slovenian ZEKom-2 and § 25 of the German TDDDG).

Every entry above is either needed for something you started yourself (signing in, choosing a language, choosing fewer animations) or needed to deliver the current version of the page you opened. None of them is used for analytics, advertising or tracking, and none is shared with anyone. For that reason the website does not show a consent banner.

Embedded content and links#

  • The website does not embed third-party content that sets cookies. Fonts, scripts, videos and demos are served from our own domain.
  • Links to Discord, Tebex or other sites take you to those sites, which have their own cookie policies.

Your choices#

  • Signing out removes the sign-in entries.
  • You can delete all stored values at any time in your browser's settings for this website. The site keeps working; you may need to sign in and choose your language again.

If this changes#

If we ever add analytics, embedded media or anything else that is not strictly necessary, we will ask for your consent first, with equal options to accept or reject, and update this page before it goes live.
