Services

Video editing

Trailers, promos, YouTube content and short clips for TikTok and Reels, edited to your style and your audience.

Updated 24 October 2026

What we need from you#

  • The type of video: trailer, promo, YouTube, TikTok or Reels.
  • Length and format: for example 60 seconds, 16:9 or 9:16.
  • Your footage, and how you will send it.
  • References you like, if you have any.
  • A deadline, if there is one.

Footage, music and rights#

  • You confirm that you have the right to use the footage, logos, music and other material you send us, and to publish the finished video where you plan to.
  • Music and stock material we add are stated in the quote, together with where the video may be published. Platforms can block or claim videos with music they cannot match to a licence, so we only use material whose licence covers your use.
  • Gameplay from GTA V and FiveM servers is subject to Rockstar Games' and Cfx.re's rules. We do not present a video as approved by them.

Revisions and delivery#

  • The quote states how many revision rounds are included.
  • We deliver the final file in the format agreed in the quote, in your ticket or through a download link.
  • Keep your own copy. We do not promise to keep project files after the ticket closes, unless the quote says so.

Using the finished video#

You may publish the finished video on your channels and platforms. What else you may do with it, and how we may show it in our portfolio, is described in Licensing and intellectual property.
