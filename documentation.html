Who you are dealing with#

Venera Studios is the brand under which our two founders, amke and daka, build FiveM scripts, Discord servers and video edits. The person or business that legally provides a service, and that you enter into a contract with, is the operator named in the Legal notice.

Operator details

The operator's legal name, address and contact email are being finalised. They will be published in the Legal notice before the Tebex store opens. Until a field is filled in, this documentation shows it as pending instead of guessing.

How we work, in short#

  • Everything is agreed in writing. Every order starts with a ticket on our Discord. The scope, price and delivery time are written into a quote in that ticket, and nothing starts or is charged until you accept it.
  • You keep a record. When a ticket closes you receive its full transcript by direct message.
  • We never ask for passwords. Staff will never ask for your password, bot tokens, licence keys or payment details in direct messages.
  • Your statutory rights stay. Nothing in our terms takes away rights you have under consumer law.

The store#

The Tebex store is not open yet. When it opens, ready-made scripts will be sold through Tebex, which acts as the reseller (merchant of record) for those purchases. The opening is announced in the announcements channel on Discord.
