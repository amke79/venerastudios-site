Legal

Licensing and intellectual property

This page explains who owns what we create and what you may do with it. Different products come with different rights, so they are described one by one. Your quote or the product page can grant more; it never takes away what the law allows you.

Updated 24 October 2026Version 2026-10-24Next review before the store opens

Who owns what#

  • Our work. We keep the copyright and other rights in the code, designs, graphics, texts and other material we create, unless your quote says that rights are transferred to you. You receive a licence.
  • Your material. Footage, logos, ideas and other material you give us stay yours. You allow us to use them only to carry out your order.
  • Other people's work. FiveM and Cfx.re, Grand Theft Auto V and other Rockstar Games content, Discord, Tebex, frameworks and open-source libraries belong to their owners. We do not own them and cannot license them to you (section F).

In some countries the author's rights can only be licensed, not transferred as a whole. Where a quote speaks of a "transfer of rights", it means the widest exclusive licence the applicable law allows.

A. Ready-made scripts from the store#

Unless the product page says otherwise, you receive a licence that is:

  • non-exclusive: we may sell the same product to others;
  • non-transferable: you may not pass it on, sell it or give it away;
  • limited to the servers stated on the product page: if no number is stated, one FiveM server, meaning one server instance identified by one Cfx.re server key that you operate;
  • valid for commercial servers: you may use it on a server that accepts donations or sells perks, as far as the rules of Cfx.re and Rockstar Games allow;
  • without a time limit, as long as you keep to these terms.

You may change the open parts of the code (for example configuration, translations and integration files) for use on your own licensed servers.

You may not:

  • share, resell, rent, lend, publish or "leak" the product or parts of it, in public or in private;
  • remove, bypass or break its protection (such as Cfx.re asset escrow), or decompile it, except where the law expressly allows it;
  • use its code in other products, especially products you sell or share;
  • remove author notices from the code or files;
  • use the product to train artificial intelligence systems.

B. Custom scripts and other commissioned software#

Unless the quote says otherwise:

  • You receive a perpetual, non-exclusive licence to use the delivered software on the servers you operate, including the source code we deliver to you.
  • You may change the delivered code for your own servers.
  • You may not resell or redistribute it to others.
  • We keep the right to reuse general techniques, libraries and parts that are not specific to your project, for example our bridge layer.

Exclusive rights (only you may use the software) or the widest transfer of rights the law allows can be agreed in the quote, usually at a different price. Third-party parts (section F) are never exclusive.

C. Discord server builds#

  • The server is yours, and so is its content.
  • Our structure, texts, banners, panel designs and templates used on your server: you may keep using them on that server, and change them, without a time limit. You may not resell them or use them as a template to sell to others.
  • A bot we host for you is a service, provided for the time stated in the quote. Its software is not transferred to you unless the quote says so.

D. Video edits#

  • You may publish the finished video on your own channels and platforms, without a time limit, and use it to promote your project.
  • Your footage stays yours. Music, sound effects and stock material we add are licensed only for the use stated in the quote.
  • We may show the finished video, or short parts of it, in our portfolio and on our channels, unless you ask us not to.

E. Updates and changes#

Updates we provide fall under the same licence as the original product. Your changes to our code are yours to use under the same licence, but the licence conditions still apply to the code underneath.

F. Third-party software and assets#

  • Our products run on FiveM and Grand Theft Auto V and may depend on frameworks such as ESX, QBCore, Qbox or ox_core, and on libraries such as ox_lib. Each of them comes with its own licence and terms, which apply to you directly. Nothing in our licence overrides them.
  • Open-source parts we include are listed with their licence in the product or delivery. Where an open-source licence gives you more rights for that part, it applies.
  • We sell and license only work that we created or that we have the right to license. We do not resell resources made by others.

When a licence ends#

  • A licence ends automatically if you break its terms, for example by sharing the product. You must then stop using the product and delete it.
  • A licence also ends when a purchase is refunded.
  • Rights you have under consumer law are not affected.

Reporting an infringement#

  • If you see one of our products shared or sold without permission, please tell us in a ticket.
  • If you believe something we publish infringes your rights, write to or open a ticket, with a description of your work and of what you believe infringes it. We look into every report and remove material that infringes.
