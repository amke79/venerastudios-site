Working with us

Ordering and quotes

Custom work is ordered through a ticket on our Discord. This page explains each step, what a quote contains, and what happens if plans change.

Updated 24 October 2026

The steps#

  1. Open a ticket. Join our Discord↗, go to open-a-ticket and choose the service: FiveM script, Discord server or video editing.
  2. Fill in the short form. It asks only what we need to understand the job, for example the framework of your server or the type of video. You do not need to have everything planned.
  3. We ask and estimate. We reply in the ticket with questions, and then with a written quote.
  4. You accept the quote. The quote has an Accept quote button. Only the person who opened the ticket can accept it, and the time of acceptance is recorded in the ticket. Nothing is started or charged before that.
  5. Payment. How and when to pay is stated in the quote (see below).
  6. We build and keep you updated in the ticket.
  7. Delivery and review. We deliver in the ticket, you check the result, and we make the changes the quote includes.
  8. The ticket closes and you receive the full transcript by direct message.

What a quote contains#

  • Scope: what we build, and what is not included.
  • Price: the total price and the currency, and whether any tax is added.
  • Delivery: the estimated delivery time, counted from acceptance or from when we receive what we need from you.
  • Notes: payment terms (for example a deposit and the rest on delivery), the number of revision rounds, and anything else agreed.

Accepting a quote means you accept the quote and the Terms of Service. Read the Refunds and cancellation policy before you accept.

Seller identity on quotes

Each quote will also name the seller (the operator in the Legal notice). This is added together with the operator details.

Payment#

  • The payment method and timing are written in your quote. Ready-made scripts in the store are paid through Tebex.
  • We never ask for card details, passwords or payment in direct messages. Payment instructions are only ever given inside your own ticket.
  • If a payment goes wrong or is taken twice, tell us in your ticket and we will sort it out (see Refunds and cancellation).

Timelines#

Delivery times are estimates. They depend on getting the information, materials and access we need from you on time. If something delays the work, we tell you in the ticket and agree a new date with you.

Changes and extra work#

  • Changes within the agreed scope and revision rounds are included.
  • New features or larger changes are a change request. We tell you the extra price and time first, and do the work only after you agree in the ticket.

Cancelling#

You can cancel an order. What you pay depends on how far the work has got, and consumers have statutory rights that always apply. See Refunds and cancellation.
